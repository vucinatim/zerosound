The suite covers:

- arbitrary split/coalesced TCP frames and malformed/truncated bounds;
- explicit control/audio join state, authenticated UDP registration, and authoritative roster
  publication;
- atomically single-use, independently expiring UDP registration leases, including wrong-token,
  replay, and expiry rejection;
- abrupt disconnect cleanup, eight-member joining, source transfer, and non-source join/leave during
  live audio;
- authenticated in-session UDP path replacement without leaving the room or changing stream state;
- silent TCP join timeout and a clean retry through the same connection owner, with stale timeout
  fencing;
- coordinator exit, successor promotion, survivor reconnection, clock relock, fresh stream generation,
  and resumed UDP audio;
- deterministic discovery convergence on the highest coordinator term with stable equal-term
  coordinator and endpoint tie-breaks;
- stale authority/source/generation fencing and prevention of competing coordinator sessions;
- bounded latest-value audio sends and the exact 1,008-byte production datagram budget;
- 20,000-packet audio-plane saturation while reliable room control remains responsive, plus 16
  repeated graceful/abrupt transport lifecycles with complete cleanup and fresh stream fencing;
- packet loss, reorder, burst concealment, underrun recovery, output-device recovery, and fresh
  room-time re-anchoring;
- five deterministic 90-second fault seeds with independent source, room, and output-clock skew,
  random single/burst loss, and live clock-map corrections, all ending below 3 ms software phase;
- two deterministic four-hour virtual soaks with independent clocks, randomized loss bursts, and
  repeated live room-clock corrections;
- backwards-compatible decoding of diagnostics from previous app versions.
